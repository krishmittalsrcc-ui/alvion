# Editing — how ALVION cuts

Sources: Krish's edit notes across client jobs, the sound-design-engine skill, and
current short-form practice (CapCut/TikTok editing guides, loudness guides, 2026).

## Cutting
- Cut on **measured** pauses, never on round numbers. Trim into silent handles and
  leave ~0.15–0.2s of air.
- Dead air kills retention. Long pauses, repeated phrases and slow starts all go.
- **Fast pace**: remove internal pauses longer than ~0.35s (jump cuts), and alternate a
  subtle punch-in (≈108%) on every other jump so the cut reads as intentional.
- Micro-cuts (0.4–1.2s) in the first few shots wake a scroller. After the hook, change
  something on screen every 3–5 seconds.
- B-roll sits *inside* the ad: keep the talking clip's audio running and cut only the
  picture away (an L-cut). Never cut B-roll with its own dialogue.

## Smooth cuts
- **Every cut gets a 5–10ms audio fade** at both ends. Clicks and pops come from audio
  starting or stopping mid-waveform; beyond ~50ms the fade becomes audible blur.
- **Sound arrives before picture**: let the next clip's audio start 6–12 frames before
  the picture cut (J-cut). The cheapest way to make generated clips feel like one film.
- A short picture crossfade (0.15–0.25s) hides a jarring jump between two similar
  framings. Hard cuts stay the default — a transition on every cut looks amateur.
- Different rooms have different room tone. Lay a consistent ambience bed under
  talking segments so the cut doesn't reveal itself in the noise floor.

## Captions
- 1–4 words per card; one clause at a time; never three lines.
- Word-by-word highlight (the current active word in accent colour) holds attention.
- **Safe zones** (1080×1920): TikTok hides ~320px at the bottom and ~120px at the right;
  Reels hides ~420px at the bottom and ~220px at the top. The universally safe band is
  roughly 12%–72% of frame height with ~6% side margins.
- If styled captions fail to render, fall back to plain white with a black outline.
- Spell-check captions against the approved script — a typo is a hard fail for clients.

## Music and sound
- Build order: foley → room tone → the one diegetic event → music → voice.
- **Music enters late** — after the hook, at the complication — not at 0:00.
- Duck music under speech (sidechain), about −12 to −18dB under dialogue.
- Give the film **one beat of true silence**: right before the turn or before the CTA.
- Don't land every cut on a beat — it flattens the rhythm.
- Inverted mute test: play the audio alone. If nothing changes over the runtime, there
  is no sound design yet.

## Loudness
- Master to **−14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP true peak**. Platforms turn hotter masters
  down; −14 is the practical target across TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## Look — making generated footage read as camera footage
- Order matters: **light blur → fine grain → grade**. Blur after grain smears it back to
  plastic.
- Grain: fine and temporal, a few percent — "smaller than you expect".
- A touch of vignette and slightly reduced saturation takes the digital edge off.
- Grade clips to match each other — the same white balance and contrast across the ad.
- UGC should look like a phone: never a cinematic grade, never beauty smoothing.
- Over-graded skin kills credibility in beauty and skincare.

## Privacy
- Faces of non-consenting or "redacted" people are blurred with a caption such as
  "identity redacted for privacy" when the brief asks for it.
