# Story, hooks and category — how the ad earns attention

## Hooks come first
Generate hooks before concepts. The first two seconds are the reason the ad exists.

Every hook runs on a named attention mechanism:
motion · transformation · extreme macro · contrast · unexpected object · impossible
angle · incompleteness · direct address · satisfying physical event · pattern break.

Hook rules: motion in frame 1; legible at thumbnail size; works muted; **no logo or
title card in the first two seconds**; no setup; connected to the product; never
over-promise.

What actually wins in paid feeds (from real CTR splits): open on **a claim about the
viewer**, never on a question the viewer can fail. "Something is living in your
stomach rent-free" beats "Are you not hungry in the morning?". Pattern interrupts
("Do NOT buy this") and avatar match (the creator looks like the buyer) beat polish.

## Structure (short-form, scales 15–60s)
1. **Situation** — first ~15%. Arrive already in motion.
2. **Complication** — to ~60%. Escalate by information, not volume.
3. **Turn** — 60–75%. The one beat that must be perfect.
4. **Residue** — last 10–15%. An image or a gesture, never an explanation.

For direct-response: hook → problem → agitation → product → proof → offer → CTA.
Proof must land in the **front half**. The product should be on screen by ~5s.

Cost: fill "____ gave up ____ in order to ____." If it resists, there is no story.

## Category physics (state it in one line at planning)
- **Food & drink** — sensory event (pour, sizzle, bite) as the hook; macro; sound first.
- **Beauty & skincare** — texture and visible result; UGC beats polish; never over-grade skin.
- **Supplements & wellness** — the objection stated flat, or the problem state; founder/
  creator trust; no medical claims; nothing pharmaceutical-looking.
- **Fashion & apparel** — fit on a real body in motion; the garment is the whole ad.
- **Jewellery** — light travelling across metal; near-silence works.
- **Fragrance** — the only category where demonstration is impossible; mist in hard light.
- **Tech** — the capability in the first second; a number on screen.
- **Home** — the transformation, hard cut; scale against a human.
- **Apps** — the real screen, fast.
- **Fintech/insurance** — a face and a plain number; no comedy.
Tangible → demonstrate. Intangible → a face. Impulse price → sensory hook.
Considered price → objection-first hook.

## Kill-room check (run on every plan)
Judge like a media buyer spending her own money. Score 0–5, default 3, anything above
3 needs a written reason: stop · hold · product clarity · visual proof · desire ·
rememberability · action · renderability.
Product clarity or visual proof below 3 → rebuild. Total below 28/40 → do not produce.
Swap test: if a competitor's product drops in and nothing breaks, the concept is generic.
Never invent predicted CTRs or hold rates.
